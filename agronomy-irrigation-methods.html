Irrigation Methods

Agronomy · Water Management · Notes for BSc/MSc/PhD, AFO, JRF/NET & state exams

Major irrigation methods: surface (border, check basin, furrow), sprinkler and drip. Drip irrigation can save 40–60% water and is best for widely spaced, high-value crops; sprinklers suit light soils and undulating land.
