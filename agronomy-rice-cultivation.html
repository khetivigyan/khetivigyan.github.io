Rice Cultivation

Agronomy · Cereal Crops · Notes for BSc/MSc/PhD, AFO, JRF/NET & state exams

Rice (Oryza sativa) is the staple food crop of India, grown on about 45 million hectares. It needs 20–37 °C temperature and is raised as transplanted lowland, direct-seeded upland or deep-water crop.

Key practices: nursery of 25–30 days, transplanting 2–3 seedlings/hill at 20×15 cm; nitrogen in three splits; keep 5 cm standing water during tillering; harvest at 20–22% grain moisture.
