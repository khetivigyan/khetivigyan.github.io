B. One-line traps to watch

  • Miracle rice = IR-8 (world) but Miracle rice of India = Jaya — read the question twice.
  • Nitrate N is not banned in lowland rice — it goes in the oxidized zone; ammoniacal N goes deep in the reduced zone.
  • Booting, not tillering, is the most critical water stage.
