Wheat Cultivation

Agronomy · Cereal Crops · Notes for BSc/MSc/PhD, AFO, JRF/NET & state exams

Wheat (Triticum aestivum) is India's main rabi cereal. Sowing time is critical — mid-November in the Indo-Gangetic plains; late sowing cuts yield by 1–1.5% per day of delay.

Key practices: seed rate 100 kg/ha, row spacing 20–22.5 cm; crown root initiation (21 DAS) is the most critical stage for first irrigation.
