Khapra Beetle

Entomology · Storage Pests · Notes for BSc/MSc/PhD, AFO, JRF/NET & state exams

Khapra beetle, Trogoderma granarium, is one of the world's most feared stored-grain pests and an important quarantine pest. Larvae feed on grain surface and germ portion; they can survive long periods without food in cracks and crevices, making eradication difficult.

Management: Strict storage hygiene, sealing of cracks, heat treatment of storage structures, and fumigation.
