Entomology28 Sep 2026

Economic Threshold Level (ETL)

ETL is the pest density at which control must be applied to stop the population reaching the economic injury level. Example: 5% deadhearts for rice stem borer; 10 BPH per hill at tillering.

Entomology27 Sep 2026

Pheromone traps

Sex pheromone traps monitor and mass-trap male moths of pests like rice yellow stem borer and brinjal shoot & fruit borer. Standard monitoring density is 5 traps per hectare.

Agronomy26 Sep 2026

Cropping intensity

Cropping intensity (%) = (Gross cropped area ÷ Net sown area) × 100. A rice–wheat–moong sequence on the same field gives 300% intensity.

Agronomy25 Sep 2026

Critical stages for irrigation in wheat

Crown root initiation (21 DAS) is most critical, followed by tillering, jointing, flowering, milk and dough stages. Missing CRI irrigation causes the largest yield loss.

Plant Pathology23 Sep 2026

Koch's postulates

Four steps to prove a microbe causes a disease: constant association, isolation in pure culture, reproduction of disease on inoculation, and re-isolation of the same organism.

Soil Science21 Sep 2026

Soil pH and nutrient availability

Most nutrients are best available at pH 6.5–7.5. Acid soils fix phosphorus with Fe/Al; alkaline and calcareous soils commonly show zinc and iron deficiency, e.g. khaira disease in rice.
