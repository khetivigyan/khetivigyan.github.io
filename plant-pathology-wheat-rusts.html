Wheat Rusts

Plant Pathology · Wheat Diseases · Notes for BSc/MSc/PhD, AFO, JRF/NET & state exams

Wheat suffers three rusts: yellow/stripe rust (Puccinia striiformis) in cool areas, brown/leaf rust (P. triticina), and black/stem rust (P. graminis tritici). Management rests on resistant varieties and timely propiconazole sprays.
