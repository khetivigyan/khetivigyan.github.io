Integrated Nutrient Management

Soil Science · Soil Fertility · Notes for BSc/MSc/PhD, AFO, JRF/NET & state exams

INM combines fertilisers, organic manures, crop residues, green manures (dhaincha, sunhemp) and biofertilisers (Rhizobium, Azotobacter, PSB) to sustain soil health and productivity while cutting fertiliser cost.
